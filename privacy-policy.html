Privacy Policy for CheckIn+

Last updated: March 20, 2026

CheckIn+ ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CheckIn+ mobile application (the "App"). This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Email address
  • Login credentials (email and password)

b. Device Information

  • Device name
  • Device identifier (Device ID)

c. Location Information

We collect location data to enable check-in functionality within the App.

d. Push Notification Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token (device token)
  • Notification data such as title, body, and related metadata

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Authenticate users
  • Enable location-based check-in features
  • Send push notifications
  • Improve app reliability and performance

3. Firebase Usage

We use Google Firebase services for the following purpose:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) – to send push notifications

We do not use Firebase services such as Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Analytics, Crashlytics, or Remote Config.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

We may share limited data with:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for notification delivery

Your FCM token may be stored on our backend and associated with your account to enable notification delivery.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion

8. Children's Privacy

This App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
