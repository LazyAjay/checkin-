Check-in Plus ("we", "our", or "us") provides a location-based employee attendance and check-in solution for organizations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect user information when using the Check-in Plus mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)

This information is used for account creation, authentication, and user identification.

b) Location Information

  • Precise GPS location during check-in

Location data is collected only at the time of check-in to verify attendance authenticity and prevent fraudulent activity.

c) Attendance Data

  • Check-in timestamps
  • Location coordinates
  • Attendance history

This information is securely stored to maintain attendance records and generate reports.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Authenticate users
  • Verify employee attendance
  • Prevent fraud and misuse
  • Maintain system security
  • Improve service reliability

We do not sell user data.
