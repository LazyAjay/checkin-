Account Deletion Request - CheckIn+

Users of CheckIn+ can request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us.

How to request account deletion

Email: lazyinventor.6@gmail.com

Please include the following details:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your organization name (if applicable)

What data will be deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your location data and check-in records
  • Your device information
  • Associated account activity data

Data retention

We may retain minimal data if required for legal, security, or compliance purposes.

Timeframe

We will process account deletion requests within 7 business days after verification.
